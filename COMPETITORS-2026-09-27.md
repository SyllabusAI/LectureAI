## Institutional / accessibility channel

- **Genio Notes** (ex-Glean/Sonocent; renamed Jun 2025) is the incumbent. Department package (min 10 seats) or campus-wide, quote only; ~£12/mo individual. SOC 2 Type II (Mar 2026), HECVAT, VPAT. UC Irvine made it the sole tool Jan 2026 amid budget cuts; students complain it has no real-time transcript or speaker labels.
- **Jamworks**: flat institutional price, used at 100+ US disability offices (vendor claim); no SOC 2/FERPA/HECVAT listed.
- **Otter** is licensed per student by several disability offices (Alabama, Berkeley, Sonoma State).
- **Panopto / Echo360 / Kaltura** now ship AI summaries, quizzes and flashcards, but only for classes the instructor records and publishes, and output stays in their platform.
- Buying requirements: VPAT/ACR (WCAG 2.1 AA), HECVAT 4 (with AI/ML section), FERPA "school official" DPA, SSO/admin console, SOC 2 plan. ADA Title II WCAG deadline is Apr 26, 2027 for large public entities.

## Recording law (confirm with counsel)
All-party consent states (secondary source): CA, CT, FL, HI, IL, MD, MA, MT, NH, PA, WA.
California Ed Code §78907 bans community-college classroom recording without instructor
consent (disability exception). University policies commonly require a recording agreement
and exclude clinical/confidential sessions. **Scheduled auto-recording is the same pattern
alleged in the Otter lawsuit**, so consent controls are a must, not a nice-to-have.

## What is still unique to Syllabus (as of today)
1. **Schedule-based auto-recording on Mac.** Nobody ships it. Notability is one step away (calendar tap-to-record).
2. **Syllabus PDF to exam-scoped study guides.** Not shipped anywhere, but **Notability has announced it.** Window is closing.
3. **Output to the student's own Google Drive, filed by course.** Not found elsewhere.
4. **Notion task export.** Not found elsewhere.

## Implications
- **Pricing pressure is severe.** Free options (Google for a year, Apple) and very cheap annual plans (Goodnotes $12/yr, Notability Plus $16/yr, Otter student $80/yr) undercut $9/mo. Hours-for-money is competitive vs Turbo/Mindgrasp, but "no free tier" will be compared against free 300 min (Otter) and free 2 hr (Turbo).
- **Lead with automation and ownership, not transcription/summaries** (everyone has those).
- **Ship syllabus ingestion fast** if it is the wedge; Notability has already announced it.
- **Build consent controls before launch**: per-class opt-out, confidential-session pause, instructor notice.
- **University channel is real but heavy** (HECVAT, VPAT, SOC 2, admin console, non-Mac coverage). It's a phase-2 move, with Genio's UX gaps (real-time, speaker labels) as the opening.
